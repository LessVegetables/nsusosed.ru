Color

Flat fills with black text on them, on warm paper with a faint 28px grid. Fills are backgrounds only, never text colors. Green, yellow and red always mean match, partial and mismatch. The fills are the same in every theme; the page, surface, text and edge swatches change with the theme switch above.
